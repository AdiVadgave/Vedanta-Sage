# JSA for Electrical Isolation & Live Work (SOP-ELE-006)

**Linked SOP:** SOP-ELE-006
**Standard:** IS 5216

| # | Hazard | Likelihood | Severity | Control Measure |
|---|--------|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Electric shock, arc flash, arc blast.... | High | Critical | Permit-to-work and proven isolation before any electrical work. |
| 2 | Electric shock, arc flash, arc blast.... | High | Major | Test-before-touch with a proven voltage detector. |
| 3 | Electric shock, arc flash, arc blast.... | High | Major | Apply earthing/grounding after isolation. |
| 4 | Electric shock, arc flash, arc blast.... | Low | Catastrophic | Arc-flash boundary established; appropriate PPE category worn. |
| 5 | Electric shock, arc flash, arc blast.... | Low | Critical | Only authorised, competent electrical persons perform the work. |
| 6 | Electric shock, arc flash, arc blast.... | Low | Major | Live work prohibited except under a specific approved live-work permit. |

**Residual Risk after controls:** Low (controls must be verified before task start).
