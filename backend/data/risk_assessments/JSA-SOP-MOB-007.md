# JSA for Mobile Equipment & Vehicle Interaction (SOP-MOB-007)

**Linked SOP:** SOP-MOB-007
**Standard:** ICMM CCM

| # | Hazard | Likelihood | Severity | Control Measure |
|---|--------|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Collision, run-over, struck-by mobile eq... | Low | Critical | Enforced pedestrian / vehicle segregation and walkways. |
| 2 | Collision, run-over, struck-by mobile eq... | Medium | Critical | Positive communication before approaching mobile equipment. |
| 3 | Collision, run-over, struck-by mobile eq... | High | Critical | Proximity detection / cameras fitted and functional. |

| 5 | Collision, run-over, struck-by mobile eq... | Medium | Major | Speed limits and traffic management plan in force. |
| 6 | Collision, run-over, struck-by mobile eq... | High | Critical | Operator fatigue management and validity of operator license. |

**Residual Risk after controls:** Low (controls must be verified before task start).
