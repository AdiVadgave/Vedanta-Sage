# JSA for Lockout-Tagout (Control of Hazardous Energy) (SOP-LOTO-001)

**Linked SOP:** SOP-LOTO-001
**Standard:** OSHA 1910.147

| # | Hazard | Likelihood | Severity | Control Measure |
|---|--------|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Unexpected energisation or release of st... | High | Major | Identify all energy sources (electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal, gravity). |
| 2 | Unexpected energisation or release of st... | Low | Catastrophic | Notify affected personnel before isolation. |
| 3 | Unexpected energisation or release of st... | Medium | Major | Shut down equipment using normal stopping procedure. |
| 4 | Unexpected energisation or release of st... | Low | Major | Isolate every energy source at the disconnect point. |
| 5 | Unexpected energisation or release of st... | High | Major | Apply individual locks and durable tags; each worker applies their own lock. |
| 6 | Unexpected energisation or release of st... | High | Catastrophic | Release / restrain all stored energy (bleed pressure, block elevated parts). |
| 7 | Unexpected energisation or release of st... | High | Major | Verify zero-energy state by attempting a start (try-out) before work begins. |

**Residual Risk after controls:** Low (controls must be verified before task start).
