# JSA for Crane & Lifting Operations (SOP-CRN-005)

**Linked SOP:** SOP-CRN-005
**Standard:** IS 13367

| # | Hazard | Likelihood | Severity | Control Measure |
|---|--------|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Load drop, crane tip-over, struck-by, pi... | High | Critical | Lifting plan approved for all non-routine / critical lifts. |
| 2 | Load drop, crane tip-over, struck-by, pi... | Low | Catastrophic | Valid third-party test certificate for crane and tackle. |
| 3 | Load drop, crane tip-over, struck-by, pi... | Medium | Catastrophic | Licensed crane operator and trained rigger/signaller. |
| 4 | Load drop, crane tip-over, struck-by, pi... | Low | Critical | SWL never exceeded; load chart available in cab. |
| 5 | Load drop, crane tip-over, struck-by, pi... | Low | Catastrophic | Barricaded lift zone; no personnel under suspended load. |
| 6 | Load drop, crane tip-over, struck-by, pi... | Medium | Catastrophic | Pre-use inspection of slings, shackles, and hooks. |

**Residual Risk after controls:** Low (controls must be verified before task start).
