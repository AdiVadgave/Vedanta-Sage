# JSA for Confined Space Entry (SOP-CSE-002)

**Linked SOP:** SOP-CSE-002
**Standard:** IS 3764

| # | Hazard | Likelihood | Severity | Control Measure |
|---|--------|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Oxygen deficiency, toxic/flammable atmos... | High | Critical | Valid confined space entry permit before every entry. |
| 2 | Oxygen deficiency, toxic/flammable atmos... | Low | Major | Continuous atmospheric monitoring (O2, LEL, H2S, CO). |
| 3 | Oxygen deficiency, toxic/flammable atmos... | Low | Major | Mechanical/forced ventilation maintained throughout entry. |
| 4 | Oxygen deficiency, toxic/flammable atmos... | Low | Catastrophic | Trained standby attendant stationed at entry point at all times. |
| 5 | Oxygen deficiency, toxic/flammable atmos... | High | Major | Rescue plan and retrieval equipment available before entry. |
| 6 | Oxygen deficiency, toxic/flammable atmos... | High | Major | Isolation and blanking of all connected lines (LOTO applied). |

**Residual Risk after controls:** Low (controls must be verified before task start).
