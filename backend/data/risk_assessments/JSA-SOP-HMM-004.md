# JSA for Hot Work & Molten Metal Handling (SOP-HMM-004)

**Linked SOP:** SOP-HMM-004
**Standard:** ICMM CCM

| # | Hazard | Likelihood | Severity | Control Measure |
|---|--------|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Burns, molten metal splash, fire, explos... | Medium | Critical | Hot work permit and fire watch for all hot work. |
| 2 | Burns, molten metal splash, fire, explos... | Medium | Major | Strictly no moisture on tools, moulds, or launders contacting molten metal. |
| 3 | Burns, molten metal splash, fire, explos... | Low | Critical | Aluminised proximity suit and face shield in molten metal zones. |
| 4 | Burns, molten metal splash, fire, explos... | Low | Major | Pre-heated tools only; verify tool dryness before contact. |
| 5 | Burns, molten metal splash, fire, explos... | Medium | Major | Fire extinguishers / Class D media available at the workface. |
| 6 | Burns, molten metal splash, fire, explos... | Medium | Critical | Exclusion of non-essential personnel from the casting bay. |

**Residual Risk after controls:** Low (controls must be verified before task start).
