# SOP-MOB-007: Mobile Equipment & Vehicle Interaction

**Document Owner:** Mining HSE
**Referenced Standard:** ICMM CCM (version 2022)
**Classification:** Critical Safety Procedure

## 1. Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure defines the mandatory controls for **Mobile Equipment & Vehicle Interaction**
to protect personnel and assets across Vedanta operations.

## 2. Key Hazard
Collision, run-over, struck-by mobile equipment.

## 3. Critical Controls
- Enforced pedestrian / vehicle segregation and walkways.
- Positive communication before approaching mobile equipment.
- Proximity detection / cameras fitted and functional.
- Seatbelt use and pre-start inspection for all mobile equipment.
- Speed limits and traffic management plan in force.
- Operator fatigue management and validity of operator license.

## 4. Procedure
1. Complete pre-start inspection; report defects.
2. Confirm pedestrian segregation and traffic plan.
3. Establish positive communication before approach.
4. Obey speed limits and right-of-way rules.
5. Park safely, apply park brake, chock wheels on grade.

## 5. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Hi-vis clothing
- Helmet
- Safety footwear
- Hearing protection

## 6. Emergency Response
In the event of an incident, stop work immediately, raise the alarm, and follow the
site Emergency Response Plan. Notify the Area HSE Officer and Shift In-charge.
