# SOP-LOTO-001: Lockout-Tagout (Control of Hazardous Energy)

**Document Owner:** Electrical & Maintenance HSE
**Referenced Standard:** OSHA 1910.147 (version 2019)
**Classification:** Critical Safety Procedure

## 1. Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure defines the mandatory controls for **Lockout-Tagout (Control of Hazardous Energy)**
to protect personnel and assets across Vedanta operations.

## 2. Key Hazard
Unexpected energisation or release of stored energy during servicing.

## 3. Critical Controls
- Identify all energy sources (electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal, gravity).
- Notify affected personnel before isolation.
- Shut down equipment using normal stopping procedure.
- Isolate every energy source at the disconnect point.
- Apply individual locks and durable tags; each worker applies their own lock.
- Release / restrain all stored energy (bleed pressure, block elevated parts).
- Verify zero-energy state by attempting a start (try-out) before work begins.

## 4. Procedure
1. Obtain a work permit and review the equipment-specific isolation plan.
2. Announce shutdown to all affected personnel.
3. De-energise via the normal stop sequence.
4. Lock and tag each isolation point individually.
5. Dissipate residual / stored energy.
6. Test for zero energy (try-to-start, gauge readings at zero).
7. Perform the maintenance task.
8. Remove locks only after confirming all personnel are clear; restore energy.

## 5. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Insulated gloves
- Arc-flash suit (electrical)
- Safety helmet
- Safety footwear

## 6. Emergency Response
In the event of an incident, stop work immediately, raise the alarm, and follow the
site Emergency Response Plan. Notify the Area HSE Officer and Shift In-charge.
