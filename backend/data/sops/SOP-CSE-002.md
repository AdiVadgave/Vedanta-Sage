# SOP-CSE-002: Confined Space Entry

**Document Owner:** Operations HSE
**Referenced Standard:** IS 3764 (version 2024)
**Classification:** Critical Safety Procedure

## 1. Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure defines the mandatory controls for **Confined Space Entry**
to protect personnel and assets across Vedanta operations.

## 2. Key Hazard
Oxygen deficiency, toxic/flammable atmosphere, engulfment, entrapment.

## 3. Critical Controls
- Valid confined space entry permit before every entry.
- Continuous atmospheric monitoring (O2, LEL, H2S, CO).
- Mechanical/forced ventilation maintained throughout entry.
- Trained standby attendant stationed at entry point at all times.
- Rescue plan and retrieval equipment available before entry.
- Isolation and blanking of all connected lines (LOTO applied).

## 4. Procedure
1. Issue confined space entry permit and verify isolations (LOTO).
2. Test atmosphere from outside; record O2 19.5-23.5%, LEL <10%.
3. Start and confirm continuous ventilation.
4. Station standby attendant; establish communication.
5. Entrants log in; maintain continuous gas monitoring.
6. Evacuate immediately on any alarm; do not attempt unplanned rescue.
7. Log out all entrants and close the permit on completion.

## 5. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Full-body harness
- SCBA / airline respirator
- Gas detector
- Intrinsically-safe lighting

## 6. Emergency Response
In the event of an incident, stop work immediately, raise the alarm, and follow the
site Emergency Response Plan. Notify the Area HSE Officer and Shift In-charge.
