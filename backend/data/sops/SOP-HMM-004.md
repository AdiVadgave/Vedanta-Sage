# SOP-HMM-004: Hot Work & Molten Metal Handling

**Document Owner:** Smelter HSE
**Referenced Standard:** ICMM CCM (version 2024)
**Classification:** Critical Safety Procedure

## 1. Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure defines the mandatory controls for **Hot Work & Molten Metal Handling**
to protect personnel and assets across Vedanta operations.

## 2. Key Hazard
Burns, molten metal splash, fire, explosion from water contact.

## 3. Critical Controls
- Hot work permit and fire watch for all hot work.
- Strictly no moisture on tools, moulds, or launders contacting molten metal.
- Aluminised proximity suit and face shield in molten metal zones.
- Pre-heated tools only; verify tool dryness before contact.
- Fire extinguishers / Class D media available at the workface.
- Exclusion of non-essential personnel from the casting bay.

## 4. Procedure
1. Issue hot work permit; assign trained fire watch.
2. Inspect and pre-heat all tools contacting molten metal.
3. Verify no water/moisture ingress paths near molten metal.
4. Don aluminised proximity suit and face shield.
5. Maintain exclusion zone during tapping/casting.
6. Keep fire watch 30 minutes after hot work completion.

## 5. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Aluminised proximity suit
- Face shield + safety goggles
- Heat-resistant gloves
- Spats / leggings

## 6. Emergency Response
In the event of an incident, stop work immediately, raise the alarm, and follow the
site Emergency Response Plan. Notify the Area HSE Officer and Shift In-charge.
