# SOP-ELE-006: Electrical Isolation & Live Work

**Document Owner:** Electrical HSE
**Referenced Standard:** IS 5216 (version 2023)
**Classification:** Critical Safety Procedure

## 1. Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure defines the mandatory controls for **Electrical Isolation & Live Work**
to protect personnel and assets across Vedanta operations.

## 2. Key Hazard
Electric shock, arc flash, arc blast.

## 3. Critical Controls
- Permit-to-work and proven isolation before any electrical work.
- Test-before-touch with a proven voltage detector.
- Apply earthing/grounding after isolation.
- Arc-flash boundary established; appropriate PPE category worn.
- Only authorised, competent electrical persons perform the work.
- Live work prohibited except under a specific approved live-work permit.

## 4. Procedure
1. Raise electrical permit-to-work; identify isolation points.
2. Isolate, lock, and tag; discharge capacitive energy.
3. Prove the voltage detector, test for dead, re-prove the detector.
4. Apply earths where required.
5. Establish arc-flash boundary; don PPE category per study.
6. Carry out work; restore only after verifying clearance.

## 5. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Arc-flash suit (rated cal/cm2)
- Insulated gloves + protectors
- Face shield / hood
- Dielectric footwear

## 6. Emergency Response
In the event of an incident, stop work immediately, raise the alarm, and follow the
site Emergency Response Plan. Notify the Area HSE Officer and Shift In-charge.
